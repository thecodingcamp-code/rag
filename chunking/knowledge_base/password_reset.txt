Resetting Your Rivergate Account Password

Rivergate passwords expire every ninety days. You will get email reminders seven days and two days before expiry.

To reset a password you still remember, sign in to the account portal and choose Security, then Change Password. The new password must be at least fourteen characters and cannot repeat any of your previous five passwords.

If you are already locked out, use the Forgot Password link on the sign in page. A one time code is sent to your registered phone number. Codes are valid for ten minutes. After five failed sign in attempts the account is locked for thirty minutes, and only the IT helpdesk can unlock it sooner.
