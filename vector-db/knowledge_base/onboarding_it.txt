IT Onboarding for New Joiners

New joiners collect their laptop from the IT desk on their first morning. The machine is already imaged with the standard build, so nothing needs installing on day one.

Your manager requests accounts a week before your start date. On your first sign in you set a permanent password and enrol a phone number in the authenticator app. Both steps have to be finished before you can reach email or the code repositories.

Building access badges are handled by Facilities, not IT. If your badge is missing on day one, reception can issue a temporary badge that works for five days.
